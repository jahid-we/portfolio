Frequently Asked Questions

What is Inertia.js and why do you recommend it with Vue?

Inertia.js bridges the gap between classic server-side frameworks (like Laravel) and modern client-side Vue components. It gives you the full reactive user experience of a Single Page Application without needing to build, authenticate, and maintain a separate GraphQL or REST API.

Can you convert an existing slow web page into a reactive Vue app?

Yes. Vue can either power a full standalone SPA or be selectively mounted onto specific complex pages (like a multi-step booking calculator or interactive product filter) while leaving the rest of the site untouched.

Is Vue.js SEO friendly?

When used via Inertia.js with Server-Side Rendering (SSR) or Nuxt.js, search engines index all content just like a traditional HTML document. For purely private dashboards and admin panels, client-side Vue is ideal.
