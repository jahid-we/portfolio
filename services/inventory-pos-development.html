Solving Real Operational Challenges for Bangladeshi Merchants

Generic off-the-shelf software often comes with high monthly subscription fees, confusing interfaces in English only, and lack of support for everyday Bangladeshi business realities: customer debt ledgers (*baki khata*), multiple payment methods (Cash, bKash, Nagad), and rapid barcode checkout.

I design and build tailored POS and inventory systems engineered specifically for local operational dynamics. You get a permanent, one-time investment with complete ownership of your data, zero monthly subscription traps, and dedicated local support right here in Brahmanbaria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the POS software work offline during internet outages?

Yes. For retail stores with unstable internet, I can deploy a local offline-first setup running on your shop computer (LAN network) that syncs data automatically when internet reconnects.

Can I print receipts in Bengali?

Yes. Thermal invoice templates can be customized with Bangla text, item names, shop addresses, and custom footers (e.g. *বিক্রিত মাল ফেরত নেওয়া হয় না*).

Is there an ongoing monthly or annual fee?

No mandatory monthly subscriptions. When I build or deploy your customized system, you own the installation and data. You only pay for any future feature expansions or server hosting if you choose a cloud version.
