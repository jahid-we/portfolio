Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose Laravel instead of WordPress for my project?

WordPress is unmatched for content sites, blogs, and standard eCommerce. However, if your application requires custom mathematical calculations, complex multi-tier database relationships, custom user workflows, or high-throughput API endpoints, Laravel offers complete freedom without the overhead of CMS plugin bloat.

Can you integrate local Bangladeshi payment gateways in Laravel?

Yes. I regularly integrate SSLCommerz, bKash Merchant API, Nagad, and Shurjopay into custom Laravel applications, ensuring secure IPN (Instant Payment Notification) callback handling and transaction verification.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and upgrades for legacy Laravel apps?

Yes. I assist businesses in migrating from older Laravel versions (e.g., 7 or 8) to modern Laravel 10/11, upgrading PHP versions, refactoring slow queries, and fixing existing bugs.
