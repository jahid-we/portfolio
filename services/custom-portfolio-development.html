Frequently Asked Questions

How much does hosting a static portfolio cost?

Hosting a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages is 100% free forever with unlimited bandwidth, automatic SSL certificates (HTTPS), and zero maintenance requirements.

How do I update projects or add new case studies later?

The HTML structure is clean, modular, and well-commented. Adding a new project is as simple as copying one project card block. Alternatively, I can provide a simple content guide or update it for you on request.

Can I connect my own custom domain (e.g. yourname.com)?

Yes. I will configure your DNS records (CNAME and A records) so your custom domain points directly to your GitHub Pages repository with free SSL encryption.
